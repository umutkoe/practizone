Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Assistant Staffing in Ontario

What certifications do PractiZone dental assistants hold?

Our dental assistants hold National Dental Assisting Examining Board (NDAEB) certificates, Ontario HARP certification for radiography, and up-to-date CPR-C / BLS certification.

Can I request a Level II Dental Assistant specifically?

Yes! When placing a staffing request through our portal, you can specify Level I or Level II requirements, including specific skills like intraoral scanning, rubber dam placement, or specialized orthodontic/surgical assisting.

How much does temp CDA staffing cost?

PractiZone offers a flat booking fee of CA$59.99 for temporary dental assistant shifts, allowing practices to manage expenses transparently. Full details are available on our Pricing Page.
