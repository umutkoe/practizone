Core Deliverables & Candidate Qualifications

PractiZone front-desk administrators possess the practical, regulatory, and software capabilities essential for modern Ontario clinics.

Software Proficiency

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Receptionist Recruitment in Ontario

Which dental software programs do your receptionists know?

Our front-desk candidates have verified experience with Ontario's leading dental practice management systems, including Tracker, Dentrix, Cleardent, AbelDent, and Curve Dental. We match you with administrators already familiar with your specific office workflow.

Are PractiZone receptionists trained in Canadian insurance billing and ODA fee guides?

Yes. Our receptionists and office administrators possess in-depth experience submitting CDAnet electronic claims, tracking pre-determinations, verifying patient insurance eligibility, coordinating dual insurance plans, and reconciling co-payments according to current Ontario Dental Association (ODA) fee codes.

How fast can we book emergency front-desk coverage?

We provide same-day emergency temporary coverage across Ontario. You can request temporary relief online through our dispatch portal 24/7 or contact our recruitment specialists directly for urgent same-morning staffing needs.

How does pricing work for dental receptionist staffing?

For temporary coverage, we offer a transparent, flat CA$59.99 booking fee per shift, with competitive hourly pay paid directly to the receptionist. For permanent recruitment, we provide structured contingency placement with a replacement guarantee. Learn more on our Pricing Page.
