Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions About Temporary Dental Staffing in Ontario

How quickly can PractiZone fill an emergency same-day dental temp shift?

When a staff member calls out sick, submit your request through our online dispatch portal or contact our dispatch team. In most major Ontario regions (GTA, Toronto, Ottawa, Hamilton), we match and confirm available RDHs or CDAs within 30 to 60 minutes for immediate morning coverage.

What credentials and compliance checks do your temporary dental staff undergo?

Every candidate is pre-screened before assignment. We verify active provincial college registration (CDHO for hygienists), HARP certification for radiography, professional liability insurance, CPR/BLS certification, and validate past clinical references in Ontario dental practices.

What are the fees and billing structure for temporary dental staffing in Ontario?

We believe in frictionless, transparent billing. Clinics pay a flat CA$59.99 booking fee per temporary shift with no hidden administrative add-ons or surprises. The temporary professional is compensated at competitive prevailing market rates. Detailed fee schedules are available on our Pricing Page.

What happens if our regular staff member extends their medical or maternity leave?

We easily transition short-term emergency coverage into extended locum or planned short-term coverage. We can maintain the same temporary professional at your practice to ensure clinical continuity and patient familiarity.
