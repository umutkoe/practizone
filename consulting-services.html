Expert Practice Advisory

Comprehensive Consulting Services for Ontario Dental Clinics

Whether you are preparing for regulatory scrutiny, optimizing production KPIs, or training your front-office leaders, PractiOne delivers tailored, proven methodologies.

1. Full Dental Office Compliance & IPAC Audits

Comprehensive infection prevention and control (IPAC) audits tailored to Ontario standards. Ensure your clinic strictly adheres to RCDSO guidelines, Public Health Ontario protocols, sterilization logs, and WHMIS regulations.

  • Reprocessing & sterilization workflow audits
  • RCDSO & Public Health Ontario inspection readiness
  • WHMIS, radiation safety, and hazard assessments

2. Practice Management Consulting & KPI Analysis

In-depth financial and operational health checks. Unlock sustainable clinic growth through detailed KPI tracking, hygiene department re-activation, billing cycle optimization, and patient retention analytics.

  • Production, collection, and overhead ratio analysis
  • Hygiene department recall & retention strategies
  • Schedule optimization to minimize downtime and gaps

3. One-on-One Office Manager Coaching & Team Training

Empower your leadership with targeted mentoring. From administrative coaching and dispute resolution to high-conversion treatment coordinator and front-desk communication workshops.

  • Office manager leadership & operational coaching
  • Treatment coordinator case presentation mastery
  • Front-desk patient experience & phone conversion

4. Custom Systems, SOPs & Manual Development

Eliminate operational chaos with customized standard operating procedures (SOPs), policy manuals, and onboarding blueprints built specifically for your practice’s unique culture and workflow.

  • Tailored clinic employee handbooks & manuals
  • Step-by-step administrative and clinical SOPs
  • Efficient new hire onboarding frameworks
