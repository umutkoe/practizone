Frequently Asked Questions

Ontario Dental Recruitment & Staffing FAQs

How do permanent placement and temporary dental staffing workflows differ at PractiZone?

Temporary dental staffing is built for rapid, on-demand shift coverage—confirming vetted RDHs, CDAs, or receptionists within minutes for sick calls, vacations, or maternity leave locums at a flat CA$59.99 booking fee. Permanent dental recruitment in Ontario follows a structured talent-matching workflow including skills assessment, culture-fit screening, software proficiency checks, and direct clinic interviews for full-time or part-time roles.

How does PractiZone verify Ontario dental credentials (CDHO, ODAA, HARP)?

Before any candidate is approved for temporary or permanent placement in Ontario, our compliance team verifies active good-standing registration with the College of Dental Hygienists of Ontario (CDHO) for hygienists, Ontario Dental Assistants Association (ODAA) / Level I & II credentials and HARP radiography certification for dental assistants, current CPR/BLS certification, and valid malpractice liability insurance.

What are the steps for an Ontario dental clinic to submit a staffing or permanent hiring request?

Dental clinics can submit a staffing request in under two minutes via our online StafStar employer portal. Simply specify the role required (RDH, CDA Level I/II, or Dental Receptionist), select permanent placement, contract locum, or temporary shift dates, note your practice management software (Dentrix, Tracker, Cleardent, or AbelDent), and our Ontario recruitment team immediately begins matching qualified candidates.

How is candidate privacy protected when registering for dental jobs in Ontario?

Candidate profiles registered through the PractiZone portal are kept strictly confidential and PIPEDA-compliant. Your contact information, resume, and regulatory documentation are never publicly listed or shared with third-party marketers, and are only presented to verified Ontario dental practices when you apply for or accept a specific shift or permanent interview opportunity.
