Toronto Dental Staffing FAQs

How quickly can PractiZone dispatch a dental temp in Toronto or the GTA?

For morning emergencies and same-day absences, we typically confirm and dispatch available Registered Dental Hygienists or Certified Dental Assistants within 30 to 60 minutes across Toronto, North York, Scarborough, and Etobicoke. You can submit requests 24/7 through our dispatch portal.

Which areas in Toronto and the GTA does PractiZone cover?

What regulatory credentials and screening checks do your Toronto dental staff undergo?

Every candidate is rigorously vetted before placement. We verify active provincial college registration (CDHO for hygienists), HARP certification for radiography, CPR/BLS healthcare provider certification, active malpractice liability insurance, and validate hands-on clinical references in Ontario dental offices.

Can PractiZone assist with both temporary emergency shifts and permanent clinic recruitment in Toronto?

Yes. We offer complete recruitment solutions ranging from emergency single-shift coverage and multi-week maternity locums to permanent part-time and full-time hiring for dental hygienists, dental assistants, and front-desk receptionists.

What dental software do your Toronto candidates know how to use?

Our roster of dental professionals has hands-on proficiency with all major Canadian practice management systems, including Dentrix, Tracker, Cleardent, AbelDent, and Curve Dental, ensuring seamless day-one onboarding with minimal clinic orientation.
